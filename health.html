HEALTH AND SAFETY POLICY

In line with our mission, the primary objectives of FLOWWELL INTERNATIONAL LIMITED is to fully satisfy and exceed the expectation of our employees and clients. This is achieved by setting and exceeding the highest standard possible in our health and safety management

FLOWWELL health and safety is zero LTIs

To achieve this target, FLOWWELL'S Health & Safety Policy is to;

Ensure the Health, Safety, and Welfare of all personnel and subcontractors.

Make all employee accountable for ensuring that Health and Safety is as important to the cost effective operation of our business as any other management objective.

Ensure that our employees and the employees of subcontractors have a clear responsibility to co-operate with each other. Thus includes exercising self-discipline, maintaining a high level of awareness, preventing injuries to themselves and others and complying with statutory obligation. Incur no accidents/incidence as a result of our business activities.

Eliminate, reduce and control risks to as low as reasonably practical (ALARP)

Ensure that our health and safety management system meets and where necessary exceeds the requirements of our client and third parties.

Ensure that the health and safety management system is updated through implementation of a management system implementation plan and annual programme.

The business development executive is fully committed to the health and safety management system. In order demonstrate this, the business development executive will ensure that all employees are aware, trained and fully committed to the implementation of the health and safety policy procedure.

Flowwell's health and safety management system has been designed to meet the requirement of the policy.
