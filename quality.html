QUALITY ASSURANCE AND CONTROL POLICY

FLOWWELL INTERNATIONAL LIMITED is totally committed to the following quality assurance and control policy objectives.

Defining and documenting the quality system for all functions of the company with ISO standard.

Ensuring that services and products provided by FLOWWELL INTERNATION LIMITED are in accordance with the contract's project specification, safety and regulatory requirements.
